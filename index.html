🪔 हमारे बारे में · About Us

क्यों बनाया — Why it was made

Most study apps are built for students who already read fast, type fast, and think in English. Bharat Voice was built the other way round: for a student in a small town who is more comfortable talking than typing, and who moves between Hindi and English mid-sentence without thinking about it.

The goal isn't another chatbot. It's a tutor that will explain a concept a third time without getting impatient, generate a fresh practice paper at 11pm before an exam, and remember how you're actually performing across subjects — not just answer one question and forget you exist.

Got questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Bharat Voice, data privacy, and learning out loud.

Is Bharat Voice free to use?

Yes! Bharat Voice provides completely free access in Guest Mode so any student can ask doubts, generate sample papers, and practice without paying or providing a credit card.

How does voice recognition work with Hinglish?

Bharat Voice is specifically calibrated for Indian student speech patterns. You can speak pure Hindi, pure English, or mix both mid-sentence naturally.

Do I need to install or download anything?

No downloads or apps are required. Bharat Voice runs directly inside your web browser on Android phones, iPhones, tablets, Chromebooks, and laptops.

How is student data and privacy protected?

We do not sell student data. Guest sessions are stored only in your local browser, and Google Sign-in is only used to sync your study goals and test scores. Read our full Privacy Policy.

Security & Privacy

Your data and advertising choices

Review how Bharat Voice handles account information, browser storage, voice input, security, and advertising in the full policy disclosures.

Guest interactions, temporary topic queries, and session state are kept in your browser's local storage.

When you choose Google Sign-In, basic profile information is used to sync study profiles, flashcards, scores, and study plans.

All communication between your browser and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
